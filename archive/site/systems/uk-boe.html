United Kingdom / GOVERNANCE & NATIONAL INFRASTRUCTURE

Bank of England settlement and CHAPS

The Bank of England runs the RTGS infrastructure and operates CHAPS, distinct from Pay.UK’s retail systems.

WHO CONTROLS WHAT

Four different responsibilities

01Ownership / controlThe Bank of England is the central-bank provider of sterling RTGS and the operator of CHAPS.
02Rules / operatorThe Bank’s RTGS/CHAPS governance sets the operating framework for these systems.
03SettlementCHAPS settles individually in real time; other retail systems settle net obligations on different cycles in RTGS.
04AccessEligible non-bank PSPs may apply for RTGS settlement access but access is not an automatic grant.
