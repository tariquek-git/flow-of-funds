United Kingdom / GOVERNANCE & NATIONAL INFRASTRUCTURE

Pay.UK retail payment systems

Pay.UK operates Faster Payments and Bacs. It is separate from the Bank of England, whose RTGS accounts settle net obligations.

WHO CONTROLS WHAT

Four different responsibilities

01Ownership / controlThe operator and settlement-service provider are distinct organizations; this is the important governance boundary at this level.
02Rules / operatorPay.UK operates retail schemes. Service availability to customers does not imply an RTGS settlement account at the Bank of England.
03SettlementFaster Payments and Bacs settle net obligations through the Bank of England’s RTGS infrastructure on separate settlement cycles.
04AccessEligible institutions may access certain schemes directly while relying on another firm for settlement.
