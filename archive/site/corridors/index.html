Payment journeys

Conceptual paths show necessary functions and key differences, never invented bank-to-bank partnerships.

CORRIDORS↗

On-us transfer

When payer and recipient are customers of the same provider, the provider may update its own ledger without a new interbank transfer for that transaction.

CORRIDORS↗

Canada · CAD account transfer

Illustrates separate bank customer accounts, Interac instruction exchange and underlying bank settlement arrangements. Not a claim of a single fixed path for all e-Transfers.
