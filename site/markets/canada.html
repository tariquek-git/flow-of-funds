Canada

GOVERNANCE / OWNERSHIP TYPE

Who runs the national systems?

A national market may have one operator or multiple independent public and private operators. Group by institution first; click into each rail for its actual rules, access and settlement.

Statutory, member-funded public-purpose organization↗

Payments Canada

A single operator owns and operates Lynx and ACSS and is developing RTR. The Bank of Canada supplies the central-bank settlement accounts.

Shareholder-owned private-sector payments network↗

Interac

Interac operates e-Transfer and debit infrastructure. Its shareholders include banks, credit unions, caisses populaires and payment acquirers.

MARKET INFRASTRUCTURE

Different pieces, distinct roles.

Interac e-Transfer, ACSS and Lynx occupy different parts of the Canadian infrastructure. Payments Canada targets an RTR launch in Q4 2026; launch and participant names must be rechecked after that point.
