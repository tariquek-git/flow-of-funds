INFRASTRUCTURE

UPI and national settlement

NPCI operates the UPI network. Third-party application providers may furnish the customer interface, while banks act as payment service providers and hold customer accounts. The Reserve Bank of India operates the RTGS system. NPCI nets UPI obligations between banks and RBI's RTGS posts them as a multilateral net settlement batch to members' RTGS accounts.

WHO RUNS THE SYSTEM?

NPCI is not RBI.

NPCI is an institution-owned, not-for-profit payment-network operator. RBI is the central bank. UPI is the scheme; it is not an app, nor every payment participant’s deposit account.
