Who runs the national systems?

A national market may have one operator or multiple independent public and private operators. Group by institution first; click into each rail for its actual rules, access and settlement.

Public central-bank system with a distinctive federal structure↗

Federal Reserve payment services

Fedwire, FedACH and FedNow are separate services in the Federal Reserve payments family, not interchangeable rails.

Privately operated, bank-owned payment infrastructure↗

The Clearing House

The Clearing House operates RTP, CHIPS and EPN, offering private-sector alternatives or complements to Federal Reserve services.

Shared industry rules; public and private ACH operators↗

ACH governance across operators

ACH is a particularly useful case: Nacha governs the common rulebook, while the Federal Reserve and The Clearing House operate the two ACH processing networks.
