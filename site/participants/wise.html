What it does in this map

  • Consumer apps & wallets

    Mobile and web experiences that initiate transfers and display available balances.

  • Business APIs & embedded payments

    Interfaces that allow a business to instruct transfers or offer payments inside another product.

  • Multi-currency balances

    Shows and manages balances across currencies in one customer experience.

  • Local collection accounts

    Provides local account details or collection capabilities for incoming payments.

  • FX pricing & execution

    Quotes and exchanges one currency for another when a payment requires conversion.

  • Local account credit

    Credits the recipient through a bank or payment institution in the destination market.
