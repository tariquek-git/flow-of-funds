THE KEY DISTINCTION

What this describes

Customer or wallet value is supported by money held in a trust arrangement.

Trust beneficiary and local payout rules depend on the jurisdiction; this is not always a single pooled FBO account.
HOW IT CONNECTS

Three separate questions

What does the customer own?Claim on a bank, e-money issuer, token issuer or other entity
Where is its backing held?Bank account, investment, trust, reserve or other arrangement
Where does payment settlement occur?Central-bank books, commercial ledger or another agreed system
