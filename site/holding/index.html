Where the money sits

These seven models describe different aspects of customer claims, bank accounts, safeguarding and reserves; they can overlap.

HOLDING↗

At the central bank

Eligible participant has an account with a central bank.

MODEL
HOLDING↗

As a bank deposit

A customer has a deposit claim against a deposit-taking bank.

MODEL
HOLDING↗

In one pooled account (FBO)

A bank holds an omnibus/FBO deposit while a separate program ledger tracks individual end-user entitlements.

MODEL
HOLDING↗

Safeguarded by the company

A payment or e-money entity protects customer funds separately from its operating funds.

MODEL
HOLDING↗

Backed by permitted investments

An entity holds permitted liquid assets against customer balances.

MODEL
HOLDING↗

In trust at a local bank

Customer or wallet value is supported by money held in a trust arrangement.

MODEL
HOLDING↗

Behind a stablecoin

Reserves are usually split across several institutions. For USDC, Circle issues the token, most reserves sit in a BlackRock-managed government money market fund whose custodian is BNY Mellon, and the rest is cash at banks. Other stablecoins are set up differently.
