BALANCE MODEL / WHERE THE MONEY SITS

In one pooled account (FBO)

A bank holds an omnibus/FBO deposit while a separate program ledger tracks individual end-user entitlements.

THE KEY DISTINCTION

What this describes

A bank holds an omnibus/FBO deposit while a separate program ledger tracks individual end-user entitlements.

FBO describes an account and recordkeeping arrangement; it is not a distinct settlement asset.
HOW IT CONNECTS

Three separate questions

What does the customer own?Claim on a bank, e-money issuer, token issuer or other entity
Where is its backing held?Bank account, investment, trust, reserve or other arrangement
Where does payment settlement occur?Central-bank books, commercial ledger or another agreed system

Evidence & limits

FDIC | Pass-through deposit insurance ↗2024 · OLDER THAN 12 MONTHS
FBO is an account-recordkeeping arrangement; pass-through deposit insurance requires ownership, titling and recordkeeping conditions. An FBO label by itself is not a coverage guarantee.
