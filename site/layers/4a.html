Participants with a function in this layer

  • Visa

    Card-network rules and acceptance infrastructure, with payout and processing-related services.

  • Mastercard

    Card scheme and switching network providing authorization, clearing and settlement-related network services.

  • Swift

    Secure standardized financial messaging used by banks and market infrastructures.

  • Interac

    Operator of Canadian payment services including Interac e-Transfer. Funds stay at participating institutions.

  • Payments Canada

    Operator of Lynx, ACSS and the planned RTR in Canada.

  • Federal Reserve

    Operates Fedwire Funds, FedNow and FedACH, and holds master accounts for eligible institutions.

  • Banco Central do Brasil

    Owns and operates Pix, including SPI settlement and DICT addressing, and runs the STR.

Examples indicate documented or illustrative functional roles, not a verified direct link to every other participant on the map.
