BREAK IT DOWN

Sub-functions in this layer

5 dedicated pages
SUB-FUNCTION↗

Real-time gross settlement

Settles obligations individually through a system such as Lynx, CHAPS or Fedwire.

SUB-FUNCTION↗

Deferred net settlement

Nets multiple payment obligations and settles the resulting positions in a later cycle.

SUB-FUNCTION↗

Central-bank accounts

Provides settlement balances to eligible institutions under system-specific access conditions.

SUB-FUNCTION↗

Settlement finality

Marks the point where the relevant settlement obligation becomes irrevocable under system rules.

SUB-FUNCTION↗

Commercial-bank / on-us ledgers

Transfers between customers on the same institution’s books without requiring a new interbank transfer for that transfer.

Participants with a function in this layer

  • Payments Canada

    Operator of Lynx, ACSS and the planned RTR in Canada.

  • Bank of Canada

    Central-bank settlement account provider and overseer of key payment systems.

  • Federal Reserve

    Operates Fedwire Funds, FedNow and FedACH, and holds master accounts for eligible institutions.

  • Banco Central do Brasil

    Operates Pix components, including SPI settlement infrastructure and DICT addressing.

Examples indicate documented or illustrative functional roles, not a verified direct link to every other participant on the map.
