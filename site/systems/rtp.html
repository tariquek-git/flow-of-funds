United States / INDIVIDUAL SYSTEM

RTP network

Operated by The Clearing House, distinct from Federal Reserve’s FedNow.

WHAT IT DOES

Private-sector instant-payment network

Instant payments between banks, settled on the RTP system's own ledger. Participants' positions are backed by a prefunded account at a Federal Reserve Bank.

Don't confuse this with: It is not interoperable with FedNow merely because both support instant payments.

HOW TO READ THIS SYSTEM

Private operator; distinct network and funding arrangements

Operator

The Clearing House operates RTP. Ownership of TCH is not a prerequisite for RTP participation.

Participation

An eligible depository institution, including a credit union, can participate; third-party service providers can support connections.

Balances

Customers hold their account balances with participating institutions, not the RTP operator.

Settlement

A payment is final when the RTP system records the debit and credit to participants' positions. Those positions are backed dollar for dollar by a prefunded balance account at a Federal Reserve Bank, funded through Fedwire or the Fed's liquidity management transfer service. TCH's settlement note names the Federal Reserve Bank of New York. The account is held for funding participants and funding agents, with TCH acting as their agent. RTP is not FedNow.
