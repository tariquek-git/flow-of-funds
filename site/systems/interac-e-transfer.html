SETTLEMENT MAP / REFERENCE
Canada / INDIVIDUAL SYSTEM

Interac e-Transfer

Interac operates e-Transfer; participating financial institutions provide the account relationships and customer balances.

WHAT IT DOES

Domestic account-to-account transfer service

Distinct consumer service, connectivity arrangements and underlying clearing/settlement mechanisms

Don't confuse this with: Interac neither holds the transferred balance nor provides an independent central-bank settlement account to every app.
GOVERNANCE CONNECTION

Who runs it?

Interac ↗

Shareholder-owned private-sector payments network

Interac describes a diverse shareholder base of financial-services entities. Shareholding is not the same as e-Transfer participation, and not every bank is necessarily a shareholder.

HOW TO READ THIS SYSTEM

Customer funds belong at participating financial institutions, not Interac

Scheme / operator

Interac operates e-Transfer, manages service rules and provides messaging capabilities.

Connection / access

The disclosure distinguishes direct connectors, indirect connectors and downstream entities.

Customer funds

Interac says it does not hold customer funds. Its terms describe funds held by the sending institution pending deposit.

Settlement

Underlying interbank clearing and settlement arrangements must not be conflated with the Interac message or funds-availability event.
