NATIONAL SYSTEMS / FIELD GUIDE

Who actually runs a payment system?

Start with ownership, rule-setting, operation, access, customer balances and settlement. Then open a national system to see how they combine.

A payment rail is not a single institution. The owner, network operator, settlement account holder and provider holding the customer's money may be different parties.
