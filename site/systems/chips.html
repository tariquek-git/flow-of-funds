United States / INDIVIDUAL SYSTEM

CHIPS

The Clearing House operates CHIPS for high-value interbank USD payments.

WHAT IT DOES

Private-sector high-value payment clearing

High-value USD payments between banks. The funds backing them sit in an account at the Federal Reserve Bank of New York, held for all funding participants.

Don't confuse this with: CHIPS is not Fedwire, even though banks use both for high-value USD payments.

HOW TO READ THIS SYSTEM

Liquidity-saving settlement is not simply Fedwire

Operator

The Clearing House operates CHIPS as a private-sector high-value USD system.

Participants

Direct CHIPS participants are banking institutions with settlement and funding obligations.

Liquidity

The liquidity-saving algorithm matches and offsets payment obligations intraday.

Finality

A payment is final when CHIPS releases and settles it on the CHIPS system's own records. Those positions are backed by the CHIPS prefunded balance account at the Federal Reserve Bank of New York, held for the joint benefit of funding participants. The Clearing House instructs that account as their agent and does not own the balances.
