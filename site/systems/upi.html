India / INDIVIDUAL SYSTEM

UPI

NPCI owns and operates UPI; banks and third-party app providers perform different functions.

WHAT IT DOES

India’s interoperable instant payments network

Instant customer payments. NPCI nets the obligations between banks and settles them in batches through the banks' RTGS accounts at RBI.

Don't confuse this with: The app the customer uses, the bank account and the network operator should be separate nodes.

Not-for-profit, institution-owned payment network operator
