The Clearing House

The Clearing House operates RTP, CHIPS and EPN, offering private-sector alternatives or complements to Federal Reserve services.

WHO CONTROLS WHAT

Four different responsibilities

01Ownership / controlThe Clearing House identifies itself as owned by major commercial banks. Ownership alone does not imply that a bank must be an owner to participate in each system.
02Rules / operatorEach of RTP, CHIPS and EPN has its own technical arrangements, operating requirements and payment characteristics.
03SettlementRTP and CHIPS each settle on their own system records, backed by prefunded accounts at the Federal Reserve that TCH instructs as agent for participants. TCH does not hold those balances. EPN is an ACH operator under Nacha rules.
04AccessOwnership, scheme membership, system participation and access through service providers are distinct.
