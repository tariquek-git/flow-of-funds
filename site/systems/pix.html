HOW TO READ THIS SYSTEM

Pix, DICT and SPI are related, but different objects

Scheme / rules

BCB owns Pix and sets the rulebook.

Addressing

BCB operates DICT to resolve aliases to transaction accounts.

Account holder

The end-user transaction account is maintained by a participating financial or payment institution.

Final settlement

SPI settles between different direct participants; indirect participants depend on a settlement intermediary.
