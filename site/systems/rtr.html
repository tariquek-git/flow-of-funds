Real-Time Rail (RTR)

Payments Canada’s proposed national instant-payment infrastructure, separately governed from Interac e-Transfer.

WHAT IT DOES

Planned instant-payment system

Planned direct participation and settlement-access arrangements

Don't confuse this with: As of October 30, 2026, the operator’s published launch target is not proof of live operation.
GOVERNANCE CONNECTION

Who runs it?

Payments Canada ↗

Statutory, member-funded public-purpose organization

Payments Canada is a not-for-profit organization created by federal law and funded by members. It is not a shareholder-owned private bank network.

HOW TO READ THIS SYSTEM

Published eligibility is not live participation

Owner / operator

Payments Canada owns and is developing RTR.

Access

Membership and system participation requirements apply; settlement-account eligibility has been published.

Customer balances

A PSP offering RTR access is not necessarily itself the deposit or e-money balance holder.

Settlement

RTR’s planned interbank settlement must not be drawn as live until launch is confirmed.
