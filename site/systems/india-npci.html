India / GOVERNANCE & NATIONAL INFRASTRUCTURE

NPCI / UPI

NPCI owns and operates the UPI network; banks act as PSPs and account providers, while customer-facing third-party apps can be separate.

WHO CONTROLS WHAT

Four different responsibilities

01Ownership / controlNPCI is a not-for-profit organization with a shareholder base including banks and other authorized payment-sector institutions; do not treat NPCI as the RBI.
02Rules / operatorNPCI owns the UPI network and prescribes its participant operating arrangements.
03SettlementNPCI is the clearing and settlement agency. It calculates each bank's net position and submits it to RBI's RTGS as a multilateral net settlement batch, which debits and credits members' settlement accounts at RBI. The customer sees the money instantly, while interbank settlement happens later, in cycles, on a net basis.
04AccessTPAP, PSP bank, issuing bank, beneficiary bank and NPCI are distinct roles. App adoption is not direct central-bank account access.
