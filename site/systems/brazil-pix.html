Brazil / GOVERNANCE & NATIONAL INFRASTRUCTURE

Pix / Banco Central do Brasil

Pix’s scheme owner and key infrastructure operator is the same central bank, unlike the separately governed networks in some other markets.

WHO CONTROLS WHAT

Four different responsibilities

01Ownership / controlBanco Central do Brasil (BCB) is the scheme owner; this refers to governance of Pix, not ownership of deposit balances held at participant institutions.
02Rules / operatorBCB defines the Pix rulebook, supervises scheme participation and operates the DICT alias directory.
03SettlementBCB operates SPI, its instant-payment RTGS settlement infrastructure. Direct and indirect SPI participation must be distinguished.
04AccessA payment institution can offer Pix through indirect settlement; customer access to Pix does not prove direct SPI membership.
