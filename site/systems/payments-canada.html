SETTLEMENT MAP / REFERENCE
Canada / GOVERNANCE & NATIONAL INFRASTRUCTURE

Payments Canada

A single operator owns and operates Lynx and ACSS and is developing RTR. The Bank of Canada supplies the central-bank settlement accounts.

WHO CONTROLS WHAT

Four different responsibilities

01Ownership / controlPayments Canada is a not-for-profit organization created by federal law and funded by members. It is not a shareholder-owned private bank network.
02Rules / operatorPayments Canada owns and operates Lynx and ACSS, including associated rules and standards.
03SettlementEligible direct participants hold settlement accounts on Bank of Canada books. Payments Canada does not itself become the central bank.
04AccessMembership eligibility is distinct from being a direct participant and from actually holding a settlement account.
STRUCTURE AT A GLANCE

Payments Canada

Statutory, member-funded public-purpose organization

One operator can run multiple rails. Distinguish its systems rather than pretending all payment methods have identical timing, access or settlement.

DRILL DOWN

Its systems and capabilities

High-value / time-critical payment settlement↗

Lynx

Payments Canada owns and operates Lynx. Direct participants must maintain eligible Bank of Canada settlement accounts.

Retail batch clearing and settlement↗

ACSS

Payments Canada operates ACSS for retail payment items such as direct deposit, pre-authorized debits and cheque-related payments.

Planned instant-payment system↗

Real-Time Rail (RTR)

Payments Canada’s proposed national instant-payment infrastructure, separately governed from Interac e-Transfer.
