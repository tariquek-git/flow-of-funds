Canada

Statutory, member-funded public-purpose organization↗

Payments Canada

A single operator owns and operates Lynx and ACSS and is developing RTR. The Bank of Canada supplies the central-bank settlement accounts.

Shareholder-owned private-sector payments network↗

Interac

Interac operates e-Transfer and debit infrastructure. Its shareholders include banks, credit unions, caisses populaires and payment acquirers.

United States

Public central-bank system with a distinctive federal structure↗

Federal Reserve payment services

Fedwire, FedACH and FedNow are separate services in the Federal Reserve payments family, not interchangeable rails.

Privately operated, bank-owned payment infrastructure↗

The Clearing House

The Clearing House operates RTP, CHIPS and EPN, offering private-sector alternatives or complements to Federal Reserve services.

Shared industry rules; public and private ACH operators↗

ACH governance across operators

ACH is a particularly useful case: Nacha governs the common rulebook, while the Federal Reserve and The Clearing House operate the two ACH processing networks.

Brazil

Central-bank owned scheme and infrastructure↗

Pix / Banco Central do Brasil

Pix’s scheme owner and key infrastructure operator is the same central bank, unlike the separately governed networks in some other markets.

United Kingdom

Industry-governed retail payment infrastructure↗

Pay.UK retail payment systems

Pay.UK operates Faster Payments and Bacs. It is separate from the Bank of England, whose RTGS accounts settle net obligations.

Central-bank owned and operated settlement infrastructure↗

Bank of England settlement and CHAPS

The Bank of England runs the RTGS infrastructure and operates CHAPS, distinct from Pay.UK’s retail systems.

India

Not-for-profit, institution-owned payment network operator↗

NPCI / UPI

NPCI owns and operates the UPI network; banks act as PSPs and account providers, while customer-facing third-party apps can be separate.
