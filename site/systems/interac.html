Canada / GOVERNANCE & NATIONAL INFRASTRUCTURE

Interac

Interac operates e-Transfer and debit infrastructure. Its shareholders include banks, credit unions, caisses populaires and payment acquirers.

WHO CONTROLS WHAT

Four different responsibilities

01Ownership / controlInterac describes a diverse shareholder base of financial-services entities. Shareholding is not the same as e-Transfer participation, and not every bank is necessarily a shareholder.
02Rules / operatorInterac runs e-Transfer operational and network services; financial institutions can be direct connectors, indirect connectors or downstream entities with different arrangements.
03SettlementInterac does not itself hold e-Transfer customers’ balances; financial institutions provide accounts and clearing/settlement arrangements.
04AccessThe public disclosure distinguishes direct and indirect connectors, participants and downstream entities.
