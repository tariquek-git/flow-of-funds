SETTLEMENT MAP / REFERENCE
Canada / INDIVIDUAL SYSTEM

ACSS

Payments Canada operates ACSS for retail payment items such as direct deposit, pre-authorized debits and cheque-related payments.

WHAT IT DOES

Retail batch clearing and settlement

Retail clearing with net settlement obligations at the Bank of Canada

Don't confuse this with: Batch retail processing should not be labelled a real-time gross settlement rail.
GOVERNANCE CONNECTION

Who runs it?

Payments Canada ↗

Statutory, member-funded public-purpose organization

Payments Canada is a not-for-profit organization created by federal law and funded by members. It is not a shareholder-owned private bank network.

HOW TO READ THIS SYSTEM

Retail clearing and final settlement are distinct events

Owner / operator

Payments Canada operates the ACSS retail batch system.

Participants

Direct clearers and indirect relationships have different roles.

Balances

Customer deposits remain obligations of account providers.

Settlement

Net interbank obligations ultimately settle using Bank of Canada accounts.
