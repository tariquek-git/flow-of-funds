Federal Reserve payment services

Fedwire, FedACH and FedNow are separate services in the Federal Reserve payments family, not interchangeable rails.

WHO CONTROLS WHAT

Four different responsibilities

01Ownership / controlThe Federal Reserve System is not a conventional shareholder-owned corporation; the Board is a federal agency and regional Reserve Banks have a distinct statutory structure.
02Rules / operatorFederal Reserve Banks operate core payment services subject to applicable Federal Reserve rules and procedures.
03SettlementFederal Reserve accounts settle relevant obligations. Direct service access and use of a correspondent/settlement agent must be evaluated separately.
04AccessFedNow participants, settlement agents, liquidity providers and certified technology service providers have distinct roles.
