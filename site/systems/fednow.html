United States / INDIVIDUAL SYSTEM

FedNow

Federal Reserve-developed infrastructure used by eligible participating financial institutions and technical providers.

WHAT IT DOES

Instant account-to-account payment infrastructure

Instant processing and settlement through appropriate Reserve Bank account arrangements

Don't confuse this with: A software vendor certified for FedNow is not thereby a settlement account holder.

HOW TO READ THIS SYSTEM

Federal Reserve service; institutions are not all the same role

Operator

The Federal Reserve Banks operate FedNow.

Participants

Financial institutions may send / receive, while other institutions may provide liquidity or settlement-agent services.

Technology

Certified technology providers support banks but do not acquire a settlement-account privilege by certification.

Settlement

Interbank settlement occurs through the Federal Reserve service using eligible participant arrangements.

Function-level explanation. Do not infer any private bilateral arrangement from this schematic. Primary source ↗
