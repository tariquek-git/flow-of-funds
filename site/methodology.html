How to read this map

A practical reference to payment infrastructure. The visual is deliberately simple; market access, sources and structural exceptions are not.

A function is not a payment path

The nine named layers classify what participants do; they are not nine steps every payment must pass through.

Five relationships remain separate

Who owns the system, who governs it, who operates it, who can access it, and who settles the obligations may be five different institutions.

Customer balances are not settlement balances

The customer claim, where backing assets are held, and the settlement asset can be legally and operationally different.

A scheme is not necessarily a settlement system

Pix and SPI, Swift messaging and interbank settlement, Interac and Lynx are distinct objects.

Eligibility is not live access

Rule changes, applications, approvals, operational launch and actual route usage must be displayed separately.

Illustrations are not confirmed bilateral routes

Without a primary source for a named sponsor, correspondent or payout relationship, the drawing shows a function, not an asserted partnership.

Coverage is not complete

These are the currencies in the original research scope. “Prototype” means this site has an educational market example; it does not mean all access relationships have been verified. “Pending” means no substantive market page exists yet.

Evidence and change

Primary sources are preferred. “Checked October 30, 2026” is the review date, not a publication date. Where dated materials are older than 12 months, they must be treated as historical unless reconfirmed. Every proposed or illustrative route remains labelled. Sources do not verify relationships they do not explicitly describe.
