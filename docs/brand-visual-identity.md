# Flow of Funds brand and visual identity

## Brand idea

Flow of Funds makes invisible payment infrastructure visible. Its identity should feel like a payment-system exhibit prepared by a precise analyst, then made approachable for the Fintech North community.

The most ownable asset is the constellation: it is simultaneously the Fintech North mark, a network, a payment graph, and a teaching device. Crimson identifies the North Star and primary action. Gold is reserved for value moving through a flow. Charcoal carries structure and explanation.

## Brand architecture

1. **Fintech North**: parent community and trust source.
2. **Flow of Funds**: product name and primary experience.
3. **Constellation**: product mnemonic and functional diagram language.
4. **Polaris**: optional conversational guide, used only when guidance is present.

The product should never make users decide whether Fintech North, Flow of Funds, the constellation, or Polaris is the main brand.

## Visual system

- **Palette**: cream and paper grounds, charcoal text, one crimson brand accent. Gold is semantic and means money in motion. Other colors only communicate payment-leg meaning.
- **Typography**: Archivo for product UI and headlines. JetBrains Mono for rails, timing, URLs, identifiers, and short system signals.
- **Shape**: compact 8 to 16 pixel radii for product surfaces. Pills are reserved for filters and tags.
- **Linework**: warm hairlines and graph connections. Avoid decorative gradients, glass, blue fintech surfaces, or generic dashboard effects.
- **Motion**: the flow assembling is the signature motion. Other transitions should be short and functional.

## Voice

Plain, precise, and evidence-aware. Explain what the payment does before naming technical machinery. Prefer `curated`, `KB-backed`, `partially sourced`, and `AI-generated` over `audited`, `verified`, or `grounded` unless a dated human review record exists.

## Current strengths

- The constellation directly demonstrates the product instead of acting as unrelated decoration.
- Crimson, charcoal, cream, and semantic gold are distinctive without falling into navy-and-neon fintech conventions.
- The beginner-to-expert detail ladder gives the brand a useful behavior, not only a visual style.
- Archivo and JetBrains Mono create a credible analyst-and-system pairing.

## Current risks and remedies

### Product naming

The previous landing lockup showed Fintech North but not Flow of Funds. The revised lockup now names both. Keep this parent/product relationship consistent in Studio, exports, metadata, and social cards.

### Mascot competition

Polaris previously appeared in the prompt and the hero caption, competing with the constellation and product name. Reserve Polaris for contextual guidance or chat. Generic creation controls belong to Flow of Funds.

### Trust language

Source availability is not the same as factual review. Every badge should be derived from active-flow evidence, with human reviewer and review date left empty until they exist.

### Social identity drift

The former share card used older lowercase naming and generic copy. The share-card generator now mirrors the product name, current promise, and curated-versus-AI distinction.

### Editorial-template risk

The cream page, mono kicker, large grotesque headline, and dot grid can become a familiar AI-generated editorial pattern. The constellation, payment-specific language, and functional evidence states must remain the dominant signatures. Keep decorative texture subtle and avoid adding more kickers, oversized type, or generic card grids.

## Release checklist

- Fintech North and Flow of Funds appear together at first contact.
- The primary CTA uses crimson; gold is used only for money movement.
- Every flow states whether it is curated or AI-generated.
- Sources shown belong to the active flow, not the entire library.
- No human review, accuracy, or global-coverage claim is inferred.
- Landing, Studio, export, PWA icon, and social card use the same product name and mark.
- Body copy reaches WCAG AA contrast; information is never color-only.
